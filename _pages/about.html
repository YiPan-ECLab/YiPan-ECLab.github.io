About Me

I am Yi Pan, an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Zhejiang University. Previously, I studied Computer Science at Central South University.

My research interests lie in WAM, vision-language-action models, world models, and embodied intelligence. I am interested in how predictive understanding of the physical world can support perception, decision-making, and action in intelligent agents.

Research Interests

  • World–Action Models (WAM): Connecting predictive world models with action generation and decision-making.
  • Vision–Language–Action (VLA): Grounding visual perception and language in robust, adaptable robot behavior.
  • World Models: Learning physical dynamics to support prediction, planning, and interaction.
  • Embodied Intelligence: Bringing perception, reasoning, and control together in agents that interact with the physical world.
